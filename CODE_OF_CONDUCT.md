# Code of conduct

## The standard

Be decent. This project exists to help people in danger, and that purpose sets
the tone for how we treat each other.

Expected: courtesy, good faith, accepting review feedback gracefully, focusing
on what is best for the people who will rely on this app.

Not accepted: harassment of any kind, personal attacks, demeaning comments,
publishing anyone private information, or sustained disruption.

## Extra care in this project

Issues and discussions here will sometimes involve domestic abuse, stalking and
medical emergencies. Discuss threat models plainly and without sensationalism,
and never use a real person situation as an example without their consent.

## Enforcement

Report concerns through the repository Security tab or to the maintainers
directly. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit, remove or
reject contributions that breach this document, and may ban repeat offenders.

Decisions are made by the maintainers and are final within this project.
